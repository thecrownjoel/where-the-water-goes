# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, welcome people of every background and level of
experience, and criticize work rather than people. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
accepted, in the repository or anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

- On GitHub, use **Report content** on the comment, issue or pull request (the "…" menu). The
  report is private: it goes to the maintainers, or to GitHub.
- For anything else, open an issue that says only that you need to reach a maintainer privately,
  without details, and a maintainer will contact you.

Maintainers will review every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and respond as the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe: from a private warning up to a permanent
ban. A maintainer involved in a report steps out of handling it.
