## Wanted

The project is looking for co-maintainers, especially people who can:

- **test and fix the Windows screensaver** (`.scr`, Screen Saver Settings, the `/p` preview) on real
  hardware;
- **test the Linux packages** and idle mode on X11 and Wayland desktops;
- **run the native macOS `.saver`** inside System Settings on macOS 14 through 27, with more than
  one monitor;
- **review data handling** with a hydrology background (groundwater, lake datums, SWOT, the National
  Water Model).

Start with [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md) and the `help wanted` issues.
